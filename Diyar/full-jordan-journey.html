For travellers who want to see it all without rushing. From the Roman north and the mosaics of Madaba to Petra, Wadi Rum and the Red Sea, every day is private and paced to leave room to breathe.

Day by day

A rhythm that suits your family.

A sample flow — the final plan shifts around your dates, pace, and the comfort level you choose.

Day 1

Amman

Arrival, the Citadel, and downtown at an easy pace.

Day 2

Jerash & Ajloun

The Roman city and a hilltop castle among green hills.

Day 3

Madaba, Mount Nebo & Dead Sea

Byzantine mosaics, sweeping views, and an afternoon float.

Day 4

The Siq, the Treasury, and an optional hike to the Monastery.

Day 6

Wadi Rum

A jeep safari, sunset tea, and a night in the desert.

Day 7

Aqaba or return

A Red Sea morning in Aqaba, or a private transfer to the airport.

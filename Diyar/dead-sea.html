Overview

The Dead Sea

The Dead Sea sits over 400 metres below sea level, and its water is so mineral-rich you cannot sink. It’s equal parts novelty and pure relaxation — a float, a mineral-mud ritual, and a resort day to recover between the desert and the road.

It’s the perfect soft landing or gentle finish to a Jordan trip, and a favourite with families.

The moments worth planning around.

  • A mineral-mud treatment and resort spa
  • Sunset over the hills of the West Bank
